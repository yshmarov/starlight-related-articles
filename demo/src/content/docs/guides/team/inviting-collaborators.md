---
title: Inviting collaborators
description: Add people to a project and choose what they can change.
---

Projects are shared by inviting people by email address. An invitation is valid
for seven days.

## Send an invitation

Enter the email address and pick a role. The invitee receives a link; they need a
Nimbus account, which the link will prompt them to create.

## Roles

**Viewer** can read settings and deployment history. **Editor** can deploy and
change project settings. **Owner** can additionally manage collaborators and
delete the project. Roles apply per project, not per account.

## Removing access

Removing a collaborator revokes their sessions immediately. Anything they
deployed stays deployed; removal only affects future access.
